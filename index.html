About This Archive

India's Culinary Memory Archive documents regional cuisines, oral traditions and food histories across India's 36 states and Union Territories. Each entry draws on published research, geographical indication records and documented cultural sources.

This is a work in progress. Sources and further reading are cited within each regional chapter and food story.
